Privacy Policy for Jester Jewels

This Privacy Policy explains how Jester Jewels, developed by Cyber builder, may collect and process information when you use the application. It also explains the use of advertising and attribution services provided by third-party service providers.

Effective date: June 4, 2026

Jester Jewels is operated by Cyber builder. Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to poyogoxoh963@gmail.com.

Jester Jewels does not require registration, login, or creation of a user account. The application does not provide an account deletion feature because no user accounts are created or maintained by the application.

When you use Jester Jewels, certain technical and usage-related information may be collected automatically through third-party software development kits integrated into the application, including Google AdMob and AppsFlyer.

This information may include:

  • device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available;
  • device type, operating system version, language, and general technical information;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from technical network information;
  • application interactions, ad views, ad clicks, and session-related events;

  • diagnostic or performance information needed to operate and measure services.

Jester Jewels uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. In connection with ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and related advertising functionality, Google may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information.

Depending on applicable requirements, device settings, region, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Additional information about Google's privacy practices is available in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Attribution and measurement through AppsFlyer

Jester Jewels uses AppsFlyer for mobile attribution, installation measurement, and advertising campaign performance analysis. AppsFlyer may process technical, device, installation, interaction, and attribution-related information in order to provide these services.

Additional information about AppsFlyer's privacy practices is available in the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

Information processed in connection with the application may be used to:

Cyber builder does not sell user personal data. Cyber builder does not operate a user account database for Jester Jewels. Information collected through Google AdMob and AppsFlyer may be stored and retained by those service providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies, contractual terms, configurations, and applicable law.
